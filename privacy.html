Privacy Policy

Status: Gym Workout Tracker for iPhone. Last updated November 17, 2026.

The short version

Status keeps your workouts on your iPhone. The developer doesn't collect, store, sell, or see your data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.

What's stored, and where

Your programs, logged sets, notes, body weight, measurements, progress photos, profile answers (such as goals, experience, and any sore spots or injuries you mark or describe), and settings are stored on your device. They are shared only with the app's own widgets and Live Activity. Deleting the app deletes them. Device backups you make through Apple may include them.

On-device AI

Build with AI, the weekly review note, and reading your injury note use Apple Intelligence on your iPhone, through Apple's on-device model. What you type and your training data are processed on the device. Status does not send them to the developer or to any other company, and doesn't use them to train anything. On iPhones without Apple Intelligence these features use the app's own built-in rules instead.

Injury and soreness features

If you mark a sore spot or write a note about an injury, that information stays on your device and is used only to choose and adjust exercises. Status is not a medical app and does not give medical advice. Check with your doctor or physical therapist before training around an injury.

Optional features you turn on

  • Apple Health. Saves the workouts you finish and the body weight you log to the Health app on your iPhone, and reads your body weight from Health so weight from a scale shows in your chart. It reads nothing else from Health, uses it only to show it to you, and sends it nowhere. Health data is never used for advertising.
  • Apple Calendar. Adds your planned workout days to your calendar and keeps them up to date. Status asks for full calendar access because it has to find its own events again to update or remove them. It changes only the events it added, and doesn't keep or send your other events. If that calendar syncs with iCloud, the events show on your other devices too.
  • Photos. When you choose a profile picture or add a progress photo, Status gets only the pictures you pick. They stay on your device.
  • Motion. The rest timer's animation follows how you tilt the phone. Motion data is used on the device for that animation only and is not stored.
  • Flashlight signal. Flashes the torch when a rest timer ends. iPhone asks for camera access for this because the torch is part of the camera; no photos or video are taken.
  • Notifications. Workout reminders are scheduled on your device.
  • Export, backup and import. Status creates a file only when you ask, and you choose where it goes. Files you import are read on your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple. Status receives only whether you have Status Pro, never your payment details.

Data shared with others

None. Status has no server, uses no third-party analytics or advertising software, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Status isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anyone's personal information.

Your choices

You can turn Apple Health, Calendar, notifications and photo access on or off in the app or in iPhone Settings at any time. You can export or back up everything from Profile → Settings → Your data, and deleting the app removes all of its data from the device.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page and in the app (Settings → Privacy Policy) with a new date.
