Support

Status: Gym Workout Tracker for iPhone.

Email db.prod.co@gmail.com with questions, problems or ideas. Please include your iPhone model and what you were doing when something went wrong.

Do I need an account?

No. Status works without signing up, and your workouts stay on your iPhone.

How do I back up or move my data?

Profile → Settings → Your data → Back up everything saves one file you keep. On a new iPhone, install Status and choose Restore from backup in the same place.

How do I manage or cancel Status Pro?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. To restore a purchase, open Profile → Status Pro → Restore Purchases.

Why can't I see Build with AI writing a program?

It needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on (iPhone Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri). On other iPhones the same screen builds a program from the app's own rules.

Is the injury feature medical advice?

No. Marking a sore spot only changes which exercises are suggested and how heavy. Check with your doctor or physical therapist before training around an injury.
